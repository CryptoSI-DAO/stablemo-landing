How it works

Three steps. No surprises.

1

Pick an offer

Compare live GHS rates from bonded sellers — or post a Quick Buy request at your own price. You'll see the exact GHS amount and all fees before you commit.

2

Pay by Mobile Money

The seller's USDT is locked in smart-contract escrow first. Then you pay by MoMo directly — payment details stay private until the trade opens.

The seller confirms your payment and your USDT releases instantly. One-hour windows keep every trade moving — and a pre-agreed arbiter is there if it doesn't.

Questions

Straight answers

Does StableMo hold my money?

No. Trade USDT is held by a smart-contract escrow on-chain — not by the platform, not by an operator wallet. Mobile Money moves directly between the two traders.

Who can see my Mobile Money number?

Only your counterparty and the trade's preselected arbiter — and only after the trade opens. MoMo numbers never appear in public listings and never touch the blockchain.

What happens if a trade goes wrong?

Every trade has an arbiter chosen by the offer creator and accepted by both sides before opening. They see the complete trade chat — payment references included — and rule to release the escrow or refund it. One ruling, on record.

What does it cost?

A flat 0.5% platform fee in USDT plus a 0.1% arbiter allocation per trade. Both are shown on the quote screen before you accept — the amount you see is the deal you get.
